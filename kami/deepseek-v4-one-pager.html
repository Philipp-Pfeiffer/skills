DeepSeek-V4, released April 24, 2026, does not win by brute force. It wins by making long context economically viable: a hybrid attention stack (CSA + DSA) cuts KV-cache footprint to one-tenth of V3.2, while FP4/FP8 mixed precision and the Muon optimizer keep training stable at 1.6T parameters. The result is a 1M-context model that costs less to serve than most 128K competitors.

Attention Revolution

Compressed Sparse Attention (CSA) + Heavily Compressed Attention (HCA) replace dense self-attention. Token-wise compression drops redundant KV pairs; DSA routes only to salient positions.

  • 10% KV cache of V3.2 at 1M context — the headline efficiency win.
  • 27% of V3.2's single-token inference FLOPs.
  • Long-context inference shifts from prohibitively expensive to production-viable.

Training at 1.6T

Muon optimizer + Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections (mHC) stabilize training. FP4 for MoE experts, FP8 for the rest — mixed precision at a scale previously considered unsafe.

  • 32T+ tokens pre-trained with FP4/FP8 mixed precision.
  • mHC constrains residual signal propagation across 61 layers.
  • Muon adapts per-parameter update magnitudes, preventing divergence.
