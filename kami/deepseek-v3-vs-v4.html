The Strategic Shiftwhat V4 signals about DeepSeek's direction

  • From efficiency to viability. V3 showed you could train cheap. V4 shows you can serve 1M context at prices undercutting 128K competitors by an order of magnitude.
  • Dual-model strategy. Pro targets ceiling; Flash targets speed/cost. Not "flagship + cut-down" — Flash is separately trained and competitive on most benchmarks.
  • Agent-first positioning. V4 is explicitly optimized for agentic coding (Claude Code, OpenClaw, OpenCode). The model is infrastructure for autonomous workflows, not just chat.
  • Long context as default. 1M is not a premium tier. Both variants ship it standard. The competitive axis shifts from "can it?" to "how much does it cost?"
The real delta is not the parameter count — it is the cost curve. V3 was a proof that Chinese labs under export controls could out-engineer unlimited-budget Western competitors. V4 is a proof that once you solve efficiency, capability becomes a commodity. When 1M context costs less than 128K used to, every application redesigns around it.
